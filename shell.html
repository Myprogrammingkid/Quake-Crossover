CROSSOVER QUAKE

Quake II pmove physics, ported line-for-line. Bring your own maps.

Drop Quake II's pak0.pak (baseq2) and/or Quake III's pak0.pk3 (baseq3) anywhere on this page, plus any custom map zips or pk3s. Files are read locally and never leave your browser.
MAIN MENU

Host below, or join an open game. Everyone with this page open on claude.ai sees the open games.

Hosting. For each player: make an invite, send it to them, paste their reply.

Joining? Paste the host's invite, then send them your reply.

Run node server.js (from the GitHub repo) and open the address it prints in your browser: the server hosts the match and serves the game.

Character

Load pak0.pak to choose from the Quake II player models and skins. You'll see your character in third person (H), and the real view weapon and rockets replace the placeholders.

Multiplayer: Play → Multiplayer starts a match against bots. Deathmatch is a free-for-all on any map you have, with frag and time limits, real ammo and health and respawning items; each bot wears its own player model and takes its name from it. Co-op keeps a single-player unit's monsters and gives you bot allies who follow you and fight them.

Movement: a port of id's pmove.c: ground accel 10, friction 6, stop speed 100, max speed 300, gravity 800, jump +270, air acceleration through PM_Accelerate(…, 1) (Q2's default sv_airaccelerate 0), 18-unit step-ups, the post-landing jump delay (PMF_TIME_LAND), duck, ladders, swimming, slick surfaces, and the 1/8-unit position and velocity snapping that makes jumps frame-rate dependent.

Collision: a port of CM_BoxTrace against the map's real brushes (player clip, windows, ladders), plus solid func_walls.

Maps: Quake II BSP v38 (and the remaster's extended QBSP) with WAL textures, the colormap palette, lightmaps and env/ sky boxes. Deathmatch and single-player maps both load.

Single-player logic: doors (teams, toggles, shoot-to-open), lifts, buttons, trains along path_corners, func_wall/func_explosive, trigger_once/multiple/relay/counter/always, timers, killtargets, delays, help messages, secrets, jump pads, teleporters, and target_changelevel, so walking into an exit loads the next map at the right spawn point. Monsters and items aren't spawned, and key-locked triggers open without the key. Rotating and secret doors are shown see-through and you can walk through them.

Characters: MD2 player models with PCX skins and vwep weapon models, animated from Quake II's own frame names (stand, run, attack, jump, crouch).

Weapons: all eleven, driven by Weapon_Generic's 10 Hz frame logic, so refire times, chaingun spin-up (1→2→3 shots per frame), HyperBlaster barrel rotation, the BFG's charge delay and grenade cooking (3 s fuse, 400–800 ups throw) match the original. Damage, spread and pellet counts are the deathmatch values. Self-splash knockback is 1600 × dmg / 200 (the original "rocket jump hack"), so rocket, grenade, BFG and barrel jumps all work. Weapon, projectile and debris models and sounds come from your pak0.

Quake III: .pk3 files mount like zips. Quake III maps (BSP v46) load with shader scripts (texture stage, blend mode, alpha test, scrolling, sky boxes and cloud layers), lightmaps, curved patch surfaces (rendered and collidable), liquids, jump pads (AimAtTarget), teleporters that throw you out at 400 ups, doors, lifts and Quake III items. Movement stays Quake II.

Sets: pick Built-in, Quake II or Quake III player models and weapons independently (Character section). Quake III players are three-part MD3s (legs, torso, head) joined by tags and animated from animation.cfg. Quake III weapons use PM_Weapon refire times, G_RadiusDamage linear splash and 1000 × knockback / 200, with view models, spinning barrels and muzzle flashes from the pk3. The first-person gun hangs off the hand model's tag_weapon, and its shot and switch motion are the hand model's frames, driven by the player's torso animation. Team Arena (missionpack) adds the Nailgun (15 nails, 20 damage each, 555–2355 ups), the Prox Launcher (mines stick where they land, arm after 2 s, trigger within 150 units, and time out after 20 s) and the Chaingun (30 ms refire, 7 damage, wide spread, spinning barrel). Their models and sounds load from the missionpack pak0.pk3 when it's mounted.

Ragdolls: when anything dies, its current animated mesh is bound to a 16-point Verlet skeleton (or a rigid box for flyers and other non-humanoids) and falls, tumbles, floats in water and reacts to bullets and explosions. The model's own death animation plays on top: it's laid onto the body's torso each frame and pulls the limbs toward it, fading out as it ends, while gravity, collisions and hits stay physical (a blast or a burst of fire loosens it). Bodies slow down smoothly before they come to rest, and old ones are frozen in place rather than removed. When you die, the camera rides the head.

Water: anything that crosses a liquid surface, including you, monsters' shots, rockets, bullets, debris and bodies, makes a splash with droplets, spray, expanding ripples, foam and bubbles. Water surfaces have animated normals, fresnel reflection and highlights. Underwater explosions throw up a plume and rockets leave bubble trails.

Enemies: every Quake II monster spawns from map entities (skill flags respected) with its MD2 model, skins (including the damaged skin), animations and sounds from your pak0. They see you (field of view and line of sight), hear your gunfire, chase you with step-up and ledge checks, fly or swim, patrol path_corners, and use their real attacks: blaster, shotgun and machinegun soldiers, gunner grenades, tank and Iron Maiden rockets, the gladiator railgun (dodgeable, aimed where you were), melee for berserkers, mutants, parasites and brains, and BFG-firing bosses. Attack odds follow M_CheckAttack. They flinch, die, fire their death targets (so kill-gated doors work), take splash knockback and gib.

Player: health, armor (jacket, combat and body absorption), falling damage, pain and death sounds, health, armor, Quad and Invulnerability pickups, and keys. Weapons and ammo are always fully stocked.

Sound: everything is read from sound/ and the players/ sexed sounds in your paks. It's positional with Quake II's distance falloff, target_speaker ambience loops in place, entering water muffles the mix, and the map's CD track plays from music/trackNN.ogg if you add the music files.

Explosions: rockets, grenades, barrels (misc_explobox, with chain reactions), func_explosive and target_explosion spawn layered fireballs, lingering smoke, bouncing sparks, embers, tumbling debris, scorch marks, dynamic light, camera shake and a screen flash. Bullet holes and rail spirals are drawn too.
